# Premium Route 11 research brief

Canonical package: `dataset/routes/route-11-hidden-halls-brass-nights-moonshot-run/`

Route 11 is the premium one-way Boston-to-Houston concept, with New Orleans as the protected required stop. It moves through Yale's Kahn galleries, the Barnes wall ensembles, Baltimore's working industrial memory, Grand Caverns, the Bristol Sessions, Biltmore, an exact underground-metal bill in Atlanta, EJI's Legacy Sites, Orthodox Sunday in Mobile, Walter Anderson's Gulf Coast folk-modernism, two nights of New Orleans history, float-making and live music, a quiet Cajun swamp, and a Houston finale built around Johnson Space Center and Rothko Chapel.

The measured road baseline is 2,141.3 miles and 44 hours 12 minutes. Days 2-8 and 11 break the original 210-minute comparison cap; the user's premium one-way exception is stored separately with a 330-minute baseline ceiling. The longest days are Sunday October 11 at 321 minutes and Friday October 9 at 301 minutes. OSRM has no live traffic, so these values are planning baselines rather than promises.

The package contains 28 rateable places and 84 reviewed local carousel images. Seventeen core places feed Magic scoring and eleven route-tested replacements remain excluded until selected. Weather is NOAA historical normal data in Celsius and retains a reduced 5% weight. All four travelers have equal votes plus person-specific route scores.

The route assumes both one-way rentals are returned in Houston and October 15 is a Texas flight or onward-travel day. It does not return to Boston Airport. Primary operational locks are the delayed-transfer cutoff on October 4, Biltmore's early entry and hard departure, final details for the October 9 Atlanta metal show, Mobile parish confirmation, Whitney's Monday-only placement, the exact October 12 Frenchmen lineup, Lake Martin weather and boat inventory, NASA's working-facility access, and every exception day's two-car live ETA.
