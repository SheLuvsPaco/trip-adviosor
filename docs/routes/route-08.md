# Route 08 research brief

Canonical package: `dataset/routes/route-08-lemurs-stone-bridges-mechanical-dreams-loop/`

Route 08 is the warmer North Carolina loop. Durham is the endpoint because a private four-person Duke Lemur Center Behind the Scenes tour gives the distance a singular purpose. The route's other defining anchors are Dia Beacon, GoggleWorks, Natural Bridge, Petersburg National Battlefield, Hotel Greene, a full Orthodox Sunday in Richmond, Hagley, the Met Cloisters and the Gardner Museum after both cars are returned.

The measured road baseline is 1,620.1 miles. All 11 core days and all 11 one-for-one replacements pass the 210-minute OSRM baseline cap. Days 1, 2, 5 and 6 remain live-traffic-gated. The app must never present OSRM as traffic-aware.

The package contains 26 rateable places and 78 reviewed local carousel images. Fifteen core places feed Magic scoring; eleven replacements remain excluded until selected. The scoring model gives expected weather a reduced 5% weight and retains equal votes for all four travelers plus person-specific route scores.

Primary operational blockers are the Duke private-tour reservation, October 12 holiday confirmation at Hagley, the October 11 Richmond parish bulletin, Hotel Greene's Saturday wait/age policy and returning both cars before the Gardner visit.
