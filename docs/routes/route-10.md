# Route 10 research brief

Canonical package: `dataset/routes/route-10-temples-follies-working-machines-loop/`

Route 10 is the Roanoke architecture, faith and working-machines loop. A motorcycle museum inside a rescued mill, BAPS Swaminarayan Akshardham, Hagley's powder works, a Marine Corps museum read critically, transplanted Agecroft Hall, Taubman's sculptural building, the Roanoke Star and giant locomotives create the outbound story. Protected Orthodox Sunday, transportation design, Skylands Manor and Boston's hidden Athenaeum make the return materially different.

The measured road baseline is 1,527.9 miles. All 11 core days and all 11 one-for-one replacements pass the 210-minute OSRM baseline cap. Days 3, 5, 8 and 11 remain live-traffic-gated; days 1, 4, 9 and 10 are near-cap. The app must never present OSRM as traffic-aware.

The package contains 24 rateable places and 72 reviewed local carousel images. Thirteen core places feed Magic scoring; eleven replacements remain excluded until selected. The scoring model gives expected weather a reduced 5% weight and retains equal votes for all four travelers plus person-specific route scores.

Primary operational blockers are the short October 4 motorcycle-museum window, Akshardham visitor etiquette and tour request, Hagley's Tuesday-only fit, the first Agecroft tour, current O. Winston Link gallery scope, October 11 parish confirmation, Tuesday exterior-only access at Skylands and returning both cars before the Boston finale.

Read the route package README for the full daily table, replacements, budget, weather normals, image-rights audit and traveler-fit analysis.
