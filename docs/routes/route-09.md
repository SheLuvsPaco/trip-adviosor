# Route 09 research brief

Canonical package: `dataset/routes/route-09-kaleidoscopes-scripture-stones-secret-machines-loop/`

Route 09 is the Columbus and central-Appalachian outsider-art loop. Its identity is not a large-city checklist: the World's Largest Kaleidoscope, Scripture Rocks, Bayernhof's secret architecture and self-playing machines, Alan Cottrill's bronze studio, the Zanesville Y Bridge, Otherworld and Columcille turn individual obsessions into the route's story.

The measured road baseline is 1,677.3 miles. All 11 core days and all 11 one-for-one replacements pass the 210-minute OSRM baseline cap. Days 1, 3, 5, 7, 9 and 11 remain live-traffic-gated. The app must never present OSRM as traffic-aware.

The package contains 25 rateable places and 75 reviewed local carousel images. Fourteen core places feed Magic scoring; eleven replacements remain excluded until selected. The scoring model gives expected weather a reduced 5% weight and retains equal votes for all four travelers plus person-specific route scores.

Primary operational blockers are Bayernhof's appointment, the October 11 St Nicholas service type, Otherworld timed admission, Taber's Tuesday confirmation, the August 26 Naumkeag ticket release and the legal no-entry boundary at Hearthstone Castle. Aldrich indoor galleries are closed Tuesday and must never be promised; only its independently documented outdoor sculpture garden is a valid swap.
