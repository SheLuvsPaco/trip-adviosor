# 1. Executive decision

Route 07 has one of the strongest raw identities in the whole project, but the original schedule did not fully cash it in.

The original version had excellent ingredients — the kazoo factory, Cleveland rock history, Mack Trucks, industrial waterfalls, acoustics, the exact Sugar show — but too often converted them into the same interaction:

> enter → look → read → leave.

The rebuild keeps the major geographic arc and turns the corridor into an **industrial playground / sound-and-motion line**.

The best research did **not** produce another Route 1-style chain of ropes courses, flight simulators and generic adrenaline. Instead it produced experiences that belong specifically to this corridor:

- shoot baskets in Springfield, where basketball was born
- watch a still-working Erie Canal lock during the 2026 navigation season
- solve automated, physical adventure rooms in Syracuse
- play through Rochester’s giant museum-of-play environment rather than browse a normal museum
- walk into a belt-driven metal-kazoo factory while it is actually working
- race on ziplines connecting abandoned Buffalo grain silos
- reset on the Lake Erie shoreline
- play instruments inside the Rock Hall instead of only reading exhibits
- compete on all-you-can-play pinball machines
- attend an exact 2026 Sugar reunion show
- ride mountain bikes through **240,000+ sq ft of an old Cleveland factory**
- attend Orthodox Sunday, then spray paint during a **hands-on graffiti session inside Carrie Blast Furnaces**
- walk with headlamps through a former Pennsylvania Turnpike tunnel
- pull a lever and follow a custom Hershey bar down a conveyor
- tour a working Martin guitar factory and then play instruments in the Pickin’ Parlor
- finish the whole loop with team simulator racing in Boston

That is a Route 07 identity that Routes 01–06 do not already own.

# 2. Refined Route DNA

## **INDUSTRIAL PLAYGROUND — SOUND, MOTION & MACHINES YOU CAN USE**

The defining question for Route 07 became:

> **Can the group touch it, play it, ride it, make something with it, hear it working, compete with it, or physically enter the infrastructure?**

If the answer was only “you can read about it,” the attraction had to be unusually exceptional to survive.

### The six Route-07 pillars

1. **PLAY THE MACHINE**  
   Pinball, basketball, full-motion racing, automated adventure rooms.

2. **ENTER THE INFRASTRUCTURE**  
   Erie Canal lock, grain silos, blast furnaces, abandoned turnpike tunnel.

3. **MAKE THE SOUND**  
   Kazoo manufacturing, Martin Guitar, Rock Hall Garage, live Sugar show.

4. **MOVE THROUGH INDUSTRY**  
   Indoor mountain biking in an old factory, silo zipline racing, tunnel exploration.

5. **LET THE GREAT LAKES BREATHE**  
   Erie / Presque Isle and waterfall resets prevent the route becoming an indoor industrial marathon.

6. **COMPETE, THEN RESET**  
   Competition is frequent, but alternated with shoreline, gorge, worship and quiet trail time.

# 3. Signature memories — selected only after corridor-wide research

These are the five moments that emerged as genuine reasons to choose Route 07.

## 1. Make a metal kazoo while the old factory is actually running
The Original American Kazoo Company in Eden remains the route’s purest DNA match. The factory still uses old machinery driven from a common motor / shaft system, factory work is currently scheduled Tuesday–Thursday, and Thursday Oct 8 is therefore the correct day to go. The paid docent option is inexpensive, and the shop currently advertises a make-your-own classic kazoo option.

**Why it matters:** this is not a display about American manufacturing. The group is standing inside a surviving small-scale manufacturing process and leaves with something that makes noise.

---

## 2. Race across abandoned Buffalo grain silos
Buffalo RiverWorks’ Racing Zipline uses four ziplines connecting three abandoned industrial silos, starting roughly 110 ft up and including a rope-bridge element.

**Why it matters:** the industrial landscape is not the backdrop to the attraction — it **is the attraction structure**.

**Critical gate:** the current operator page supports weekday operation and a Summer/Fall product, but exact Oct 8 inventory must be confirmed before this is considered booking-final.

---

## 3. Cleveland becomes a playable rock-and-machine city
This is a sequence rather than one static attraction:

**Rock Hall Garage → all-you-can-play pinball → exact Sugar reunion show → Ray’s old-factory bike park next morning.**

It moves from making sound, to mechanical competition, to live amplified sound, to physical riding inside a repurposed factory.

**Why it matters:** this is the strongest “Route 07 only” city block.

---

## 4. Orthodox Sunday → spray paint inside the Carrie Blast Furnaces
Sunday Oct 11 starts with Divine Liturgy at St. Nicholas Greek Orthodox Cathedral, then moves to Rivers of Steel’s **exact-date Hands-On Graffiti Tour at 12:30 PM**. The tour is limited, advance-ticketed, lasts about 2.5 hours, explores the graffiti collection, and then lets participants try graffiti themselves.

**Why it matters:** worship, steelmaking history, postindustrial art and physical participation occur in one coherent day without feeling like a history lecture.

---

## 5. Headlamps through the Old PA Pike → handmade Martin guitars
The return line changes mechanical texture completely: first the group enters the dark, undeveloped former Pennsylvania Turnpike at Rays Hill Tunnel; the next morning it enters Martin’s working guitar factory in Nazareth.

**Why it matters:** one machine-era relic has gone silent and wild; the next production line is still making instruments by hand. It is a better mechanical-history story than adding two more transportation museums.
