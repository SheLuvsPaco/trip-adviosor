Research-complete seventh dataset entry for October 4-15, 2026. It remains entirely within the United States with no Canada or J-1 re-entry dependency.

## Why this route is different

This is the playful sound-and-machinery route. It starts with five museums in one compact Springfield campus, meets the real Samuel Wilson behind Uncle Sam in Troy, gives Gora the only sports-forward stop at the International Boxing Hall of Fame, and joins Susan B. Anthony's house to the Douglass/Anthony memory landscape at Mount Hope.

The westbound crescendo is intentionally strange: working kazoo machinery, Lake Erie ecology, Cleveland's rock archive, a movie house, a small object-led witchcraft museum and the exact Sugar show. The following morning is deliberately slow at the Cleveland Museum of Art before the cars turn south. That contrast keeps the one intense city day from defining the entire trip.

The return is a different story rather than a repeated road: a full Orthodox Sunday, the giant Bedford Coffee Pot, Mack's truck archive, a 38-foot Uncle Sam bookend in Danbury and an MIT finale only after both cars are returned. Small lodging stages in McConnellsburg and the Lehigh Valley exist to keep the route honest under the bed-to-bed driving rule.
