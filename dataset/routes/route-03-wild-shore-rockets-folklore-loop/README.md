# Route 03 — The Wild Shore, Rockets & Folklore Loop

Research-complete third dataset entry for the October 4-15, 2026 trip. The road trip departs Boston Logan on October 4, returns to a Boston Logan hotel on October 14, and protects October 15 for the airport. It is entirely within the United States and has no Canada or J-1 re-entry dependency.

## Locked route

Boston Logan → Old Lyme → New London → Greenport → Shelter Island → Riverhead → Garden City → Asbury Park → Cape May → Lewes → Berlin → Assateague → Chincoteague → Wallops → Eastern Shore → Norfolk/Virginia Beach → Richmond → Baltimore → Philadelphia → Metuchen → Sleepy Hollow/Croton → Fairfield → Boston Logan.

- 11 calendar days and 11 hotel nights, including Boston on October 14
- 1,415.6 baseline road miles; ferry mileage is separate
- 39 scheduled, separately rateable places plus 11 hidden-gem anchor replacements
- 150 visually audited, locally cached carousel images: three per place with creator, rights and source metadata
- 7 larger-city or practical metro nights and 4 quieter small-town nights
- One intentionally social night in Asbury Park, dependent on the real October calendar
- No scheduled athletic/adrenaline stop on this route
- One Orthodox Sunday morning in Richmond
- Four ferry legs: Cross Sound, North Ferry, South Ferry and Cape May-Lewes
- Eleven measured replacement variants; every baseline stays at or below the 3h30 bed-to-bed driving cap

## Why this route should feel magical

This route treats the Atlantic edge as the experience rather than dead space between cities. It moves from an American Impressionist boardinghouse to working car ferries, a tidal preserve, a duck-shaped building, lunar hardware, an unlimited-play pinball museum, Lucy the Elephant, a World War II aviation hangar, Victorian folklore, a concrete shipwreck, coastal-defense towers, wild horses, a NASA sounding-rocket center, the Chesapeake Bay Bridge-Tunnel, Edgar Cayce's unusual archive, Poe's garden, an Orthodox cathedral, two forms of industrial history, a medical-mystery museum and a full Sleepy Hollow finale.

The route is deliberately not a collection of the first famous results. Greenport and Shelter Island replace a routine Connecticut-to-New York highway day; Berlin and Cape Charles make overlooked towns part of the holiday; the Barrier Islands Center supplies the human history behind the landscape; Metuchen is used as a calm reset rather than another big-city night; and the exact-date Blaze is paired with the places that created the Headless Horseman story.

## Ratings and Magic score

Every place has four equal traveler rating slots and a 1-5 scale in 0.5 increments. The route winner uses:

- attraction and stop ratings: 45%
- excitement and uniqueness: 20%
- driving comfort: 15%
- traveler fairness: 10%
- cost and value: 5%
- expected weather: 5%

The weather weight remains intentionally small. Replacement places do not affect the Magic score until selected. Person-specific route scores remain separate so the app can show each traveler's favorite alongside the group result.

## Budget and booking order

The modeled controllable range is $1,045-$1,510 per person before lodging, cars, fuel, nightlife and personal shopping. The known admission and ferry floor is about $355 per person; two-car ferry pricing is the largest shared variable.

1. Buy the exact October 13 Great Jack O'Lantern Blaze timeslot.
2. Lock two vehicle spaces on Cross Sound Ferry and Cape May-Lewes Ferry.
3. Select the actual October 6 Asbury rock/metal bill and October 7 Cape May ghost or oddities departure when those calendars open.
4. Reconfirm Lucy's interior climb, Fort Miles Battery 519 and the October operations of every ferry.
5. Book two-room lodging—one 1-bed room and one 2-bed room—prioritizing safety, cleanliness, parking and late check-in over resort features.

## Image and deployment status

All 150 selected carousel images were visually reviewed. Of those, 102 are Wikimedia Commons images with license metadata recorded. Forty-eight exact-place external editorial images are intentionally marked `permission-required-before-public-deployment`. They are suitable for the private route-selection prototype, but a public deployment must license, replace or remove them; the dataset never presents those images as cleared stock.
