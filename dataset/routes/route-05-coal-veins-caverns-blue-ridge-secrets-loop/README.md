# Route 05 — The Coal Veins, Caverns & Blue Ridge Secrets Loop

Research-complete fifth dataset entry for the October 4-15, 2026 trip. The road trip departs Boston Logan on October 4, returns both cars and sleeps near Boston Logan on October 14, and protects October 15 for the airport. It remains entirely within the United States and has no Canada or J-1 re-entry dependency.

## Why this route should feel magical

The opening deliberately avoids a generic Connecticut transit day. The Wadsworth's Gothic castle and art cabinets lead into Holy Land USA's volunteer-restored hill of biblical miniatures, giant cross and new Stations trail. Monday then becomes the literal route thesis: 300 feet underground in the Lackawanna anthracite mine, followed by a working rail roundhouse and giant stone blast furnaces.

Pennsylvania stays specific rather than becoming a checklist. The group moves from thousands of clocks into Lancaster's real market and folklore-heavy Troll Market; then from the radical religious experiment at Ephrata to cathedral-scale locomotives, a one-lane covered bridge and Harrisburg's broken iron river span. The southbound hinge joins the Capitol's civic theater to Antietam's silence and three layers of Harpers Ferry: the postcard Lower Town, Storer College's Black education and civil-rights story, and Virginius Island's industrial ruins.

The southern half combines Luray's mirror lake and stone organ with a short Skyline segment, Stony Man and a calm Charlottesville evening. Saturday is the route's most intellectually ambitious day: Monticello's slavery-centered interpretation, Black Charlottesville at Jefferson School, Indigenous Australian art at Kluge-Ruhe, IX's immersive art and one real Virginia rock night. The return protects Orthodox Liturgy, follows with evidence-first Civil War medicine, then uses a Moravian farm machine, respectful adaptive reuse at Fairfield Hills and the Mapparium's stained-glass 1935 world as the final image.

## Ratings and Magic score

Every place has four equal traveler rating slots and a 1-5 scale in 0.5 increments. The route winner uses:

- attraction and stop ratings: 45%
- excitement and uniqueness: 20%
- driving comfort: 15%
- traveler fairness: 10%
- cost and value: 5%
- expected weather: 5%

The weather weight remains intentionally small. Replacement places do not affect the Magic score until selected. Person-specific route scores remain separate so the app can show each traveler's favorite alongside the group result.
