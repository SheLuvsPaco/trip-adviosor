# Route 04 — The Trolls, Moon Rocks & Curiosity Coast Loop

Research-complete fourth dataset entry for the October 4-15, 2026 trip. The road trip departs Boston Logan on October 4, returns to a Boston Logan hotel on October 14, and protects October 15 for the airport. It remains entirely within the United States and has no Canada or J-1 re-entry dependency.

## Locked route

Boston Logan → Portsmouth → Portland → Rockland → Bar Harbor/Acadia → Bangor → Bethel → Lancaster/Weeks State Park → St. Johnsbury → Montpelier → Burlington → Waterbury/Plymouth Notch → Woodstock → Windsor/Cornish → Manchester → Boston Logan.

- 11 calendar days and 11 hotel nights, including Boston on October 14
- 1,063.4 baseline road miles
- 37 scheduled, separately rateable places plus 11 hidden-gem anchor replacements
- 144 visually audited, locally cached carousel images: three per place with creator, rights and source metadata
- Seven larger-city or practical city nights and four quieter destination-town nights, preserving the requested roughly 65/35 split
- Two optional social nights: Arcadia in Portland and a calendar-gated Radio Bean night in Burlington
- No scheduled athletic/adrenaline anchor
- Full Orthodox Sunday morning in Burlington
- Eleven measured replacement variants; every core and replacement baseline stays at or below the 3h30 bed-to-bed driving cap

## Why this route should feel magical

This is not simply an Acadia foliage drive. It starts with an offshore lighthouse, an experimental teardrop-hull submarine and Portsmouth's African Burying Ground; moves through gilded domestic technology, a pinball night, giant forest trolls, crafts made through Maine's prison industries, a mile-long harbor breakwater and a bridge observatory; then compresses an Acadia sunrise, granite coast and the Bubbles into a short high-impact morning before cryptids, Stephen King's house and a sunset standpipe.

The inland half becomes stranger rather than quieter: Gora can hold a lunar meteorite in Bethel, everyone rides an exact-date foliage train, a Victorian cabinet of curiosities is paired with Weeks State Park's summit house, Orthodox Liturgy leads into Shelburne Museum's ship-and-building campus, and a flavor graveyard is followed by the farmhouse where a presidential oath was taken. The finale combines Quechee's gorge, the birthplace of precision manufacturing, Saint-Gaudens' art landscape and two Frank Lloyd Wright houses. The route's hidden-gem set can swap in a self-service barn museum about ordinary objects, a dog chapel covered with handwritten memories, brass-era cars, shipbuilding, raptors and a sculpture garden about the human life cycle.

## Ratings and Magic score

Every place has four equal traveler rating slots and a 1-5 scale in 0.5 increments. The route winner uses:

- attraction and stop ratings: 45%
- excitement and uniqueness: 20%
- driving comfort: 15%
- traveler fairness: 10%
- cost and value: 5%
- expected weather: 5%

The weather weight remains intentionally small. Replacement places do not affect the Magic score until selected. Person-specific route scores remain separate so the app can show each traveler's favorite alongside the group result.

6. Book two-room lodging—one 1-bed room and one 2-bed room—prioritizing safety, cleanliness, parking and late check-in over resort features.
