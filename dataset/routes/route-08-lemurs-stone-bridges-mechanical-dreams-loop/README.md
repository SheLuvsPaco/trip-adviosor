Research-complete eighth dataset entry for October 4-15, 2026. The route remains entirely within the United States with no Canada or J-1 re-entry dependency.

## Why this route is different

This is the warm southbound discovery route. Its endpoint is not a city checklist: it is a private four-person Behind the Scenes tour at Duke Lemur Center with potential aye-aye access. That singular experience is earned through a sequence of places that already feel like destinations—monumental art inside Dia's daylight factory, working studios inside a goggle factory, Shenandoah gardens and the walk beneath Natural Bridge.

The return does not repeat the outbound story. Petersburg is treated as a siege and human landscape, not a weapons display. Richmond then shifts from a fictional 1930s hotel mini-golf world to a protected full Orthodox Sunday and Poe's Enchanted Garden. Hagley's water-powered powder works, the Met Cloisters and the Gardner Museum's courtyard and factual theft trail complete the northbound arc.

Day 3 deliberately combines GoggleWorks with the route's one controlled fashion-outlet window. Hotel Greene is playful and social but is not counted as a large nightlife event. This preserves the group's energy for the private lemur tour, church and the route's larger cultural landscapes.
