# Route 09 — The Kaleidoscopes, Scripture Stones & Secret Machines Loop

Research-complete ninth dataset entry for October 4-15, 2026. The group collects two cars at Boston Logan on October 4, returns both cars and sleeps near Logan on October 14, and protects October 15 for the airport. The route remains entirely within the United States with no Canada or J-1 re-entry dependency.

- every core day and all 11 replacement variants remain at or below the 210-minute road-network baseline cap

## Why this route is different

Route 9 is the outsider-art and obsessive-craft loop. The magic comes less from famous cities and more from people who transformed unlikely objects and landscapes: a grain silo becomes the world's largest kaleidoscope, a forest becomes a field of scripture-carved boulders, a Pittsburgh mansion hides self-playing instruments and secret architecture, and a working Zanesville studio overflows with bronze figures.

Columbus is the endpoint because the sequence culminates at Otherworld, not because the route needs another generic city weekend. The Y Bridge and Alan Cottrill studio make the approach part of the story. The return changes tone completely: free American art in Youngstown, protected Orthodox worship, a quiet Victorian Bellefonte night, a modern megalith sanctuary, a legal exterior encounter with a ruined Connecticut castle and Harvard's Glass Flowers after both cars are safely returned.

This is intentionally a no-club route. It uses wonder, optical play, puzzles and conversation as the social energy, then alternates them with quiet towns so the group is not burned out by the 1,677-mile total.
